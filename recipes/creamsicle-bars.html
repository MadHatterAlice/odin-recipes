Creamsicle Bars from the Gods

Ingredients

  • 1L of your favourite Ice Cream
  • Box of your favourite biscuits

Process

  1. Microwave your biscuits until they are warm but not warped
  2. Quickly spoon as much ice cream as you can on top of one biscuit
  3. Place another biscuit on top of the ice cream
  4. Eat before it all melts!
